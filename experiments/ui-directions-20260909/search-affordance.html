Search.

Binoculars next to fuel and ride settings. Three controls in a row, just above the sheet — a quiet, consistent place that becomes the focused search experience when you need it.

Try the flow
  1. Tap the binoculars button (bottom-left, dark row)
  2. Type anything — results cascade in
  3. Tap a result — watch the pin drop
  4. Choose Route here or Waypoint
  5. Dismiss to reset — binoculars return
Why this placement works.
Fuel range and ride settings are the "how I ride" controls. Search is "where I'm going" — same row, same language, same reach. The binoculars sit dark and quiet until you need them, then bloom into the focused search overlay. One icon, one tap, one job.
